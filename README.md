# ARK-Protocol-2026: Official Technical Specification
## Deep Investment Standard (DIS) by Apollonas Soben

### 1. MISSION STATEMENT
Establishment of a global ecosystem for the protection of nature through high-value contemporary art assets. Transitioning all operations to **Solo Status** to ensure integrity, security, and direct environmental impact.

### 2. CORE FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE (DIS)
* **Valuation Index:** Fixed at 1.1 €/cm² (Euro per square centimeter).
* **Asset Class:** Strategic Environmental Investment (SEI).
* **Audit Protocol:** Every artwork is subject to a rigorous technical and psychological audit before entering the ARK ecosystem.

### 3. LEGAL & STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK: "THE RIGHT TO BE"
The "Right to Be" (Право быть) philosophy is the foundational law of this protocol. It dictates that art exists as a functional locomotive for the rebirth of life and the security of Earth's biological assets.

### 4. SOLO STATUS PROTOCOL
As of 2026, Apollonas Soben operates under **Solo Status**. This ensures:
* Complete creative and financial independence.
* Zero-party mediation.
* Direct investment into nature protection funds via art acquisitions.
